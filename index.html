About MsikaOnline

Malawi's premier online marketplace connecting buyers and sellers across the country.

Founded in 2023, MsikaOnline was created to provide a safe, reliable platform for Malawians to buy and sell goods locally. Our mission is to empower small businesses and individuals by providing easy access to markets across Malawi.

With secure payment options including MPamba, Airtel Money, and bank transfers, we make transactions simple and worry-free. Our verification system helps build trust between buyers and sellers.

Help Center

Find answers to common questions about using MsikaOnline

Account Help

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Register" button in the top navigation and fill out the form with your details. You'll need to verify your email address to complete registration.

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

On the login page, click "Forgot password" and enter your email address. You'll receive instructions to reset your password.

How do I update my account information?

After logging in, go to your account settings where you can update your personal information, profile picture, and notification preferences.

Buying Help

How do I contact a seller?

Each product page has a "Contact Seller" button that allows you to send a message to the seller through our secure messaging system.

What payment methods are accepted?

We support MPamba, Airtel Money, and bank transfers. Payment methods available depend on what the seller has enabled for their listing.

What should I do if I didn't receive my item?

First, contact the seller to inquire about the status. If you don't receive a satisfactory response within 48 hours, contact our support team for assistance.

Selling Help

How do I list an item for sale?

Click "Sell" in the main navigation and fill out the product details form. Be sure to include clear photos and an accurate description of your item.

How are payments processed for sellers?

Buyers send payments directly to you through the payment method you've specified. We recommend only shipping items after payment is confirmed.

Can I sell services on MsikaOnline?

Currently, we only support physical products. We may expand to services in future updates.

Safety Tips

How can I spot potential scams?

Be wary of deals that seem too good to be true, sellers who refuse to meet in person for local transactions, or requests to pay outside our platform.

Where should I meet for local transactions?

Choose public places like shopping centers or police stations. Avoid meeting at private residences unless you know the person well.

What should I do if I encounter a problem?

Contact our support team immediately with details of the issue. For urgent matters, call our helpline at +265 123 456 789.

Still need help?

Our support team is available to assist you with any questions or issues.
